01 THE PROGRAM

Curiosity, put to work.

From classwork
to working prototype.

In small teams, students take on an industry challenge proposed by an alumni mentor and build a working prototype over a semester.

Workshops, hands-on labs, and weekly presentations help students practice engineering tools, explain their decisions, and learn from feedback.

Learn the tools.

Practice software design, version control, AI-assisted development, and team workflows.

Build with a team.

Test ideas, share progress, and improve a prototype with feedback from alumni mentors.

Take your work further.

Practice interviews and networking. Selected teams pitch their work during a San Francisco career trek.
